Privacy Policy — ChessPath

Last updated: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

ChessPath ("we", "our", or "the app") is a chess improvement application designed for competitive players. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little data as possible.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use ChessPath, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Contact: medrunia@gmail.com

2. Data You Enter in the App

ChessPath allows you to store chess-related information directly on your device, including:

  • player profile information such as pseudonym, full name, country, federation, and chess ratings
  • chess games and PGN data
  • openings, preparation notes, and psychological notes
  • opponent information such as name, rating, style, and preparation notes
  • tournament information such as event name, dates, location, budget, and goals
  • study sessions, routines, reviews, reading lists, and training goals

This information is stored locally on your device only.

We do not provide account creation, cloud sync, or our own backend server for this data.

3. Data Processed by Third-Party Services

ChessPath uses Google services for advertising and consent management.

Google Mobile Ads SDK

When ads are shown, Google Mobile Ads may process data such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device information, diagnostic information, and user interactions related to ads. This processing is handled by Google and may be used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, security, and measurement.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

If required by applicable law, ChessPath uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect and store the user's ad consent choices. This includes consent status and related privacy preferences.

For more information about how Google processes data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions Used

ChessPath may request the following Android permissions:

Permission Reason
INTERNET Required to load ads and consent messages
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used to check connectivity before loading network-based services
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used for reminders and study session notifications
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Used to schedule study blocks, reminders, or training alerts at specific times

ChessPath does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or phone call data.

5. How Your Data Is Used

  • to store and display your chess preparation and training data locally on your device
  • to provide app features such as study planning, reminders, exports, and progress tracking
  • to display ads through Google Mobile Ads
  • to manage consent choices where required by law

We do not sell your locally stored chess data.

6. Data Sharing

Your locally entered chess and preparation data is not sent to our servers.

However, data may be processed by Google and its partners when ads or consent flows are used in the app, according to their own policies and systems.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your chess preparation data remains on your device until you delete it.

You may:

  • export your data from the app, where that feature is available
  • delete your locally stored app data through the app's settings, where that feature is available
  • uninstall the app

Please note that on some Android devices, app data may be included in system backup or restore mechanisms unless backup for that data is disabled or excluded by the app.

8. Security

We aim to minimize data collection.

Your chess preparation data is intended to remain in the app's local storage on your device and is not stored on our own servers. Data processed by Google services such as ads or consent is handled by those services under Google's own infrastructure and policies.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free, but we take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary data exposure by keeping core user data local whenever possible.

9. GDPR / EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If required by applicable law, users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland may be shown a consent form before personalized ads are displayed.

Where supported by the consent framework in use, users can review or change their privacy choices from within the app.

10. Children

ChessPath is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children through our own systems.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: medrunia@gmail.com

13. Google Play Data Deletion Clarification

ChessPath does not provide user account creation.

Because there is no account system, there is no account to delete. Instead, users can remove locally stored app data from their device through the app's settings or by clearing app storage on Android.
